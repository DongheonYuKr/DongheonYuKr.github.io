SAMPLE · fictional product

Skincare · Korean source (summary): 쌀겨 추출물이 들어간 약산성 젤 클렌저, 150ml. 거품이 부드럽고 세안 후 당기지 않음. 「피부 장벽 회복」. 아침·저녁 사용.

[Brand] Low-pH Rice Bran Gel Cleanser, 150 ml / 5.07 fl oz

  • Gentle daily gel cleanser: A clear gel that turns into a soft, light lather with a little water.
  • Low-pH formula: For morning and evening use.
  • Rice bran extract: A classic Korean skincare ingredient, inspired by the traditional rice-water rinse.
  • No tight feeling: Rinses off clean and leaves skin feeling soft, not stripped.
  • Easy-squeeze tube: 150 ml / 5.07 fl oz.

A gentle gel cleanser for every day. Work a small amount into a soft lather, massage over damp skin, and rinse. Skin feels clean and comfortable afterward.

How to use: Wet your face. Lather a pea-sized amount with water in your palms, massage gently over your face, then rinse with lukewarm water. Use morning and evening.

What I changed: the Korean source said “restores the skin barrier.” That reads like a drug claim in the US, so I didn't carry it over and flagged it instead. Volume was converted from ml to fl oz.

SAMPLE · fictional product

Body care · Korean source (summary): 무화과 향 핸드크림, 50ml, 끈적임 없이 빠르게 흡수, 가방에 넣기 좋은 크기. 「스트레스를 풀어 주는 향」.

[Brand] Fig Leaf Hand Cream, 50 ml / 1.69 fl oz

  • Soft fig leaf scent: Green and lightly sweet, not heavy.
  • Non-greasy feel: Absorbs quickly, so you can type or scroll right after applying.
  • Bag-sized tube: 50 ml fits in a pocket, a tote or a desk drawer.
  • Light cream texture: Spreads easily with a small amount.
  • Everyday use: Apply whenever your hands feel dry.

A light hand cream with a soft fig leaf scent. It sinks in fast and leaves hands feeling smooth, without a slippery film.

How to use: Squeeze a small amount onto the back of your hand and smooth it over both hands and fingertips.

What I changed: the Korean source said the scent “relieves stress.” I described the scent itself instead of claiming an effect.

What I don't do

  • Regulatory or legal judgments. Registration, labeling, safety substantiation and FDA / MoCRA compliance stay the seller's responsibility.
